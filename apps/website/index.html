One day on the beat, start to finish

Your salesman's whole route runs on one phone. Every order, bill and rupee collected reaches your books the moment it happens, or as soon as the network comes back.

  1. 9:30 am

    Plan the beat

    Today's shops, visits and reminders are waiting on the salesman's phone, with each shop's location and dues.

  2. 11:15 am

    Take the order

    Search the catalogue, tap quantities and see wholesale, retail and MRP for every item. No order books, no copying later.

  3. 11:20 am

    Print the bill

    A GST invoice or a kaccha estimate prints on a Bluetooth printer at the counter, or goes to the shop owner on WhatsApp.

  4. 6:00 pm

    Close the day

    Cash, UPI and dues are already in the khata. You see the day's sales, collections and cash in hand on your home screen.

Built for the distributor who still keeps a khata

Most distribution in India still runs on a godown, a few vans, a team of salesmen and a register full of dues. The software made for it is usually either too simple to trust with your books or too complicated for your salesmen to use.

SalesArmy takes the work that happens at the shop counter, the order, the bill and the payment, and puts it on one phone that a salesman learns in a morning. Behind that sits an accounting system that keeps your stock, dues and cash exact.

Questions distributors ask us

Can't find yours? Talk to us

Does it work where there is no network?

Yes. Orders, bills and payments are saved on the phone first and sync on their own when the network returns. Each entry is sent once, so nothing is counted twice.

Which printers can I use?

Any 58mm (2 inch) or 80mm (3 inch) Bluetooth thermal printer that supports ESC/POS, which covers most printers sold for billing. You can also buy one with your plan.

Can I give a bill without GST?

Yes. You can print a kaccha estimate with no tax, or a full GST tax invoice, for any order. Both go into the shop's khata the same way.

What happens when my trial or plan ends?

Nothing is deleted. You can still sign in, see all your data and reports, and collect dues. Creating new orders, bills and other entries starts again as soon as you renew.

Can my staff see my accounts?

Only if you allow it. Each person gets a role, and you can switch every permission on or off, from billing and collections to staff and settings.

Can I move from my current register or software?

Yes. Add your products, shops and opening dues and you can start billing the same day. Talk to us if you want help with a large catalogue.
