1. Terms of Service & Commercial Usage Agreement

Welcome to SalesArmy (the "Platform"), owned and operated by SalesArmy Technologies Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"). These Terms of Service ("Terms") constitute a legally binding agreement between you (the "Tenant", "Distributor", "Merchant", or "User") and the Company regarding your subscription to our cloud platform, mobile applications, and procurement of integrated POS hardware.

1.1 Eligibility & Account Authorization

The Platform is designed exclusively for commercial enterprise use (B2B). By creating a tenant account, you affirm that:

  • You are at least 18 years of age and possess full legal authority to enter into commercial contracts on behalf of your agency or enterprise.
  • All business registration credentials provided (including GSTIN, PAN, Firm Name, and Authorized Signatory information) are accurate, valid, and operational.
  • You will maintain the security and confidentiality of all staff account credentials, OTP verifications, and API access tokens.

1.2 SaaS Subscription & Indian GST Regulations

SalesArmy SaaS subscriptions are billed on a recurring monthly or annual basis under SAC Code 998314 (Information Technology Software Services). All subscription prices are subject to the applicable Indian Goods & Services Tax (GST) rate of 18%.

  • Input Tax Credit (ITC): Registered business tenants providing a valid 15-character GSTIN will receive automated GST-compliant tax invoices via email for claiming Input Tax Credit.
  • Auto-Renewal & Grace Periods: Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled prior to renewal. Accounts experiencing billing failures receive a 72-hour grace period before access to live dispatch order syncing is suspended.

1.3 Permissible Use & Data Sovereignty

You agree not to reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract source code from the mobile client, web admin console, or offline SQLite database engines. All transaction ledgers, beat routes, and retailer pricing data entered by you remain your proprietary commercial data.

Data Protection & Privacy

2. Privacy Policy & Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDPA 2023)

This Privacy Policy describes how SalesArmy collects, processes, stores, and protects personal and commercial data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

🛡️ Data Residency Commitment: All operational data, customer ledgers, and authentication credentials are stored on secure cloud servers situated physically within the Republic of India (Mumbai Region / ap-south-1).

2.1 Data Categories Collected

  • Identity & KYC Data: Distributor proprietor name, trade name, registered address, GSTIN, PAN, and contact numbers.
  • Operational & Field Data: Field sales agent GPS coordinates recorded strictly during active beat shifts for route optimization and geo-fenced retail check-ins.
  • Financial & Payment Records: Transaction IDs, UPI Reference Numbers, invoice amounts, and bank account settlement details. Note: Full credit card numbers or UPI MPINs are never accessed or stored by our servers; payments are processed directly via RBI-licensed aggregators (Razorpay / NPCI).

2.2 Data Fiduciary Responsibilities & User Rights

As a Data Principal under DPDPA 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access & Summary: Request an automated export of all customer records, order histories, and staff logs stored in your tenant namespace.
  • Correction & Erasure: Rectify inaccurate personal data or request tenant account decommissioning subject to statutory commercial tax retention mandates (under Section 36 of CGST Act requiring 72 months record preservation).
  • Grievance Redressal: Submit privacy concerns directly to our designated Data Protection Officer / Grievance Redressal Officer.
Billing Transparency

3. Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy

In compliance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and payment aggregator operating guidelines, our refund and cancellation provisions are established as follows:

3.1 SaaS Subscription Cancellations

  • Cancellation Notice: Tenants may cancel their monthly or annual SaaS subscription at any time directly through the Super Admin Portal or by contacting support.
  • Term Expiry: Upon cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of the current pre-paid billing cycle. No further auto-debits will occur.
  • Digital Services Refund Policy: Because cloud provisioning, database allocations, and server resources are provisioned immediately upon transaction confirmation, subscription fees for active cycles are generally non-refundable once activated. In the event of duplicate billing or erroneous double-charges, a 100% refund will be credited to the original source account within 5–7 banking days.

3.2 POS Thermal Printer Hardware Return & Replacement

For optional hardware purchases (58mm / 80mm Bluetooth Thermal POS Printers):

  • Dead on Arrival (DOA) / Defective Hardware: If your POS printer arrives physically damaged or defective, notify our support team within 7 calendar days of courier delivery along with unboxing proof. We provide an immediate free doorstep replacement.
  • Manufacturer Warranty: All POS printers carry a standard 12-Month Manufacturer Hardware Warranty covering printhead and motor performance under normal commercial usage.
  • Order Cancellation prior to Dispatch: Hardware orders cancelled prior to courier handover receive a 100% refund processed within 48 business hours.
Logistics & Delivery

4. Shipping & Hardware Fulfillment Policy

SalesArmy ships Bluetooth POS thermal printers and commercial accessories to commercial distributors across all pin codes in India.

4.1 Dispatch & Courier Partners

  • Dispatch Window: All verified hardware orders are packaged and dispatched from our central warehouse within 24 to 48 hours (excluding national holidays and Sundays).
  • Logistics Partners: Deliveries are managed through premier logistics carriers including DTDC, BlueDart, Delhivery, and India Post Speed Post.
  • Consignment Tracking: An automated SMS and in-app notification with an active AWB Tracking Number is dispatched immediately upon courier handover.

4.2 Transit Timelines

📦 Estimated Delivery Time: Metro & Tier-1 Cities: 2 to 4 business days. Tier-2 & Rural Districts: 4 to 7 business days.

In the event of unforeseen regional disruptions, transit delays exceeding 10 business days qualify for express re-dispatch or a full refund upon customer request.
